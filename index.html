How prices change by week

  • Each resort has a demand level per week (low, normal, high, peak), based on the school holidays of the countries that ski there.
  • Houses: −10% at low demand, +30% at high, +60% at peak, versus a normal week. Flights: −10%, +20%, +35%. Car and transfer: +10% at high, +20% at peak.
  • Lift passes and ski rental have fixed high-season prices (19 Dec to April), so they don't change by week.
  • With 4 snow days the Wed→Mon, Thu→Tue and Fri→Wed patterns are 5 nights and use 4 days of leave. A 5th snow day adds a night at the end (Thu→Tue becomes Thu→Wed): one more night of house, one more day of food and one more day of leave. In Easter week Good Friday (26 Mar) is a public holiday in Portugal, which brings it down to 3. Weeks marked * include Carnival Tuesday, which many employers in Portugal give off.
  • From 20 March, low resorts show a spring-snow warning.
  • Every table sorts when you tap a column header; tap again to reverse. The resort filter applies to the grid, ranking, houses, detail and comparison.
  • Many apartments in France rent Saturday to Saturday. Mid-week 5-night stays are easier to find outside the holiday weeks.

How your group changes the price

  • Groups: 4 couples + 1 friend (9 people, 4 bedrooms: the friend sleeps on a sofa bed in the living room), 4 couples (8 people, 4 bedrooms) 3 couples (6 people, 3 bedrooms), or 4 friends with no couples in their own rooms (4 bedrooms) sharing 2 rooms, or 4 separate beds in any rooms (the listing must say it has 4 beds, or have 4 bedrooms). One bedroom per couple; a house fits if it sleeps everyone and has the bedrooms needed, up to 2 more.
  • House: the median price of the houses in the chosen level that fit your group (enough bedrooms and beds). Houses are split into the cheapest, middle and priciest third. With no fitting houses listed, the price is estimated from a 2-bedroom flat, scaled by bedrooms0.9.
  • Houses are listed inside each resort in “Flights and others”. “Walk to the lifts” keeps houses whose listed distance is in the band: ski in / ski out (100 m or less), up to 5 min (375 m) or up to 10 min (750 m), at 75 m a minute in ski boots. With a band set, houses with no distance listed are left out; “All” keeps every house.
  • Rental car: one car per 5 people. Transfer: one vehicle per 8 people, and a 5–8 seat van costs about 40% more than a car transfer.
  • Food per person per day × 6 days (one more than the nights, arrival to departure), priced from each resort’s meal-out cost in the comparison: cooking at home with slope snacks is about 0.6 of a meal out, one meal out a day about 1.4, lunch and dinner out about 2.2 (the rest is groceries and snacks). Or pick Custom and set your own daily amount.
  • Flights are from Porto, direct with one checked bag. Unconfirmed: Porto–Chambéry; Porto–Memmingen (Ryanair, ~5 a week) and Porto–Turin (Ryanair) exist but may not fly on winter dates.
  • Distances are by road from the airport to the resort, without traffic; Saturdays can be much slower.
