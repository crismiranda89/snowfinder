# Data licence

The files in `data/` (resort facts, ratings, season prices, demand calendars and the curated listing
index) are © Snowfinder contributors and licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Price estimates are a point-in-time snapshot with their sources listed in the season file.

House photos are **not** part of this repository. The site shows each listing's own photo straight from
the listing's site, credited and linked back; those photos belong to their owners. Listing names, links
and the prices someone saw for given dates are recorded as facts.
